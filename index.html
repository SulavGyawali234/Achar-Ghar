About Achar

Achar is a popular traditional side dish in Nepal. It is usually prepared using vegetables, fruits, herbs and a mixture of spices. From spicy tomato achar to tangy radish and mango achar, every variety brings a unique flavor to the table.

Popular Types of Achar

🍅

Tomato Achar

Spicy and flavorful tomato achar made with roasted tomatoes, chilies, garlic and traditional spices.

🥭

Mango Achar

Tangy raw mango mixed with spices to create a delicious sweet, sour and spicy pickle.

🥒

Cucumber Achar

A refreshing and crunchy achar prepared with cucumber, sesame, chili and aromatic spices.
